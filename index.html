Margherita Pizza

A classic Italian pizza topped with fresh mozzarella, tomato sauce, basil leaves, and baked until perfectly crispy.

Prep: 20 mins
Cook: 15 mins
Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Fresh Mozzarella
  • Fresh Basil
  • Olive Oil
  • Oregano
  • Salt & Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough evenly.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the base.
  4. Add mozzarella slices evenly.
  5. Sprinkle oregano and drizzle olive oil.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes.
  7. Top with fresh basil and serve hot.
